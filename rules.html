House rules and privacy

These cover every game in The Puzzle Shed, including Tally and Letterbox. Short version: be kind, play fair, and we only keep what the games need to work.

House rules

The chats, leagues and challenges are for having fun with other players. By playing, you agree to these rules.

  • Be kind. No bullying, threats, hate or abuse of anyone, for any reason.
  • Keep it clean. No sexual, violent or shocking content, and nothing illegal.
  • Keep personal details private. Don't post your own or anyone else's address, phone number, email or other private information.
  • Pick a sensible nickname. Nothing rude or hateful, and don't pretend to be someone else.
  • No selling or spam. No adverts, links to sell things, or the same message over and over.
  • Play fair. One account each. No cheating, and no ways round the game's limits.
  • Mind the spoilers. The chats are built to hide answers from people still playing. Please don't work around that.

What happens if someone breaks the rules

Depending on how serious it is, we may delete a message, stop a nickname from posting, remove someone from the leaderboards, or delete their account. Anything that looks illegal is removed straight away, and may be reported to the police.

The Puzzle Shed is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Reporting a problem

  • In the games: tap Report under any chat message. The owner gets a notification and looks at it as soon as possible.
  • By email: tallypuzzle@gmail.com. Use this for anything else that worries you, including a nickname, a league or something you saw in a private league chat.

Complaints and appeals

If you think we got something wrong, for example a message was removed or you were stopped from posting by mistake, email tallypuzzle@gmail.com and say what happened. We'll look again and aim to reply within 7 days.

The person responsible for safety on The Puzzle Shed, and for dealing with reports and complaints, is Mathew Doughty.

Privacy

The Puzzle Shed is a free hobby project run by Mathew Doughty in the UK. You can contact him about your data at tallypuzzle@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your nickname and password. No real name or email is needed. Passwords are handled by Google's Firebase sign-in service and are never visible to us.
  • Your scores: points, guesses, times, and whether you used a clue.
  • Challenges, leagues and invites you set up or join.
  • Chat messages you post, and any reports you make.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, a code from your phone's browser so we can send them, plus your choices of what to get.

Why

Only to run the games: to show leaderboards, leagues and challenges, to send the notifications you asked for, and to keep the chats safe. We don't sell your information, show adverts or use tracking cookies.

Where it's kept

Each game stores its information with Google Firebase. The websites are hosted by GitHub Pages, and the lettering comes from Google Fonts. Like most websites, these services may briefly see your device's internet address when you visit. Your game in progress is also saved on your own phone, in the browser.

Who can see it

Your nickname, scores and leaderboard positions are public. Chats about a puzzle can be read by players who've finished it. League chats can only be read by members of that league. Your password and notification settings are private.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you'd like your account and everything linked to it deleted, or a copy of what we hold about you, email tallypuzzle@gmail.com with your nickname and we'll do it within a month.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it's used. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Thanks for playing, and for keeping the shed a friendly place.
